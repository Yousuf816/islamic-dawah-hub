Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Light of Guidance is committed to protecting your privacy. This platform is designed with privacy-first principles and minimal data collection.

Information We Collect

Minimal Data Collection: We collect only what is necessary for the site to function:

  • Local Storage: Your theme preference (dark/light mode) and tasbeeh counter count are stored locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.
  • Location (Optional): Prayer times feature requests your location for accurate times. This is optional and only used locally - never sent to our servers.
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, emails, IP addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

Third-Party Services

External APIs: We use the following services:

  • Aladhan API: For prayer times and Islamic calendar (privacy-friendly, no tracking)
  • CDN Services: Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts, Font Awesome (standard CDN usage)

These services may have their own privacy policies. We use them only for essential functionality.

Analytics (Optional)

If analytics are enabled, we use privacy-friendly analytics that:

  • Do not use cookies
  • Do not track individuals
  • Do not collect IP addresses
  • Only track aggregate usage statistics

You can opt out by setting localStorage.setItem('analytics-opt-out', 'true') in your browser console.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear all local storage data (use browser settings)
  • Disable location services
  • Opt out of analytics
  • Use the site without any data collection

Data Security

Since we don't collect personal data, there's no personal data to secure. All data stored locally in your browser remains on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our site is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our About section.
