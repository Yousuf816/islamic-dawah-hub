Through proper understanding (tafsir), we can apply the teachings of the Quran in our modern context while maintaining the purity of the message.

About Light of Guidance

Light of Guidance is a comprehensive Islamic platform dedicated to spreading the beautiful teachings of Islam through authentic sources. Our mission is to provide easy access to Quranic verses, authentic Hadith, and valuable Islamic resources for Muslims and non-Muslims alike.

Every moment spent learning, reflecting, and acting upon authentic knowledge is a form of Da'wah and worship. Our intention is solely to seek the pleasure of Allah and to share beneficial reminders in a clear and respectful way.

Disclaimer: This platform shares verses, hadith, and general reminders from widely accepted sources. It is not a replacement for qualified scholarship. For specific questions and personal matters, please consult trusted, knowledgeable scholars. Any mistakes are from us, and all correct guidance is from Allah alone.

Please remember us in your du‘ā and share only what you find authentic and beneficial.
